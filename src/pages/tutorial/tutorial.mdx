</p>


<p style="font-size: 1.5rem; margin-top: 2.5rem;">
  Once you have successfully configured the environment, the entire directory structure should be as follows:
</p>

<div style="background-color: #f8f9fa; padding: 16px; border-radius: 8px; font-family: 'Courier New', Courier, monospace; font-size: 1rem;">
  <pre style="margin: 0; color: #333;">

<p style="font-size: 1.5rem; margin-top: 2.5rem;">
  LAMM is one of the first open-source frameworks for Multi-modal Large Language Models (MLLMs) training and evaluation.
  Unlike frameworks such as LLaVA, LAMM specifically focuses on training and evaluating MLLMs for embodied agents.
</p>

<p style="font-size: 1.5rem; margin-top: 1.5rem;">
  Specifically, we have integrated data and code from a series of research projects, including:
</p>

<ul style="font-size: 1.1rem; margin-left: 2rem; margin-top: 1rem; line-height: 1.8;">
  <li>· Training and evaluation datasets for MLLMs supporting both 2D and 3D temporal data.</li>
  <li>· Training and evaluation code for MLLMs.</li>
  <li>· Implementation frameworks for embodied AI downstream tasks in both simulated environments (such as Minecraft) and real-world robotics manipulation scenarios.</li>
</ul>

<p style="font-size: 1.5rem; margin-top: 1.5rem;">
  For detailed information, please refer to our tutorials or specific research projects and documentation in the research section.
</p>

<p style="font-size: 1.5rem; margin-top: 2rem;">
  The framework provides a comprehensive solution for researchers and developers working at the intersection of multi-modal learning and embodied intelligence. 
  Our goal is to facilitate the development and assessment of MLLMs that can effectively interact with and understand physical environments.
</p>
